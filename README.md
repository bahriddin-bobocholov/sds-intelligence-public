# SDS Intelligence — Public Research & Engineering Portfolio

**Governance Intelligence | Regulatory Technology | AI Governance | Financial Crime & Compliance**

SDS Intelligence is a research and engineering initiative focused on turning governance language in contracts, policies, approval structures, exceptions, and related documents into structured, reviewable intelligence.

This repository is intentionally limited to **non-sensitive research, architecture patterns, reproducibility concepts, and synthetic examples**. Proprietary scoring logic, ontology details, adjudication data, customer-specific workflows, internal product design, and confidential materials are not included.

## What this repository demonstrates
- Evidence-linked governance intelligence
- Human-in-the-loop review patterns
- Auditability and provenance
- Reproducible analytical workflows
- AI/model governance principles
- Regulatory and financial-crime technology architecture
- Institution-level benchmarking concepts
- Secure separation between public research artifacts and proprietary platform logic

Selected public research facts:
- 288 contracts
- 4,299 clauses
- 19 publicly traded U.S. banks
- 81 bank-year observations
- Expanded 24-bank eligibility inventory with 8,708 clauses
- Frozen benchmark of 390 adjudicated clauses across 21 banks

These figures describe research datasets and benchmark infrastructure. They are **not claims that SDS predicts misconduct or future enforcement**.
